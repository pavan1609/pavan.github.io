Current focus

Research

My work sits between machine learning, sensing, and intelligent physical systems.

01

Wearable sensing

Adaptive sampling for human activity recognition

I study how deep-learning models behave when wearable sensors operate at different sampling rates, and how one model can remain useful across changing sensing conditions instead of requiring a separate model for every rate.

02

Efficient machine learning

Robust models under sensing constraints

My work includes subject-independent evaluation, model compression, and large-scale experiments across datasets to understand the trade-off between sensing cost, model complexity, and recognition performance.

03

M.Sc. thesis

Weight-space learning for adversarial robustness

My Master's thesis investigates whether robustness-related information can be transferred between neural networks through their learned parameters, including cross-dataset and cross-architecture settings.
